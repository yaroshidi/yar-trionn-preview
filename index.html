Me. I lead every project and I'm your contact from the first call to the monthly report. AI agents I trained do the repeat work, like research, early design passes and checking code before it ships. If you're a B2B tech company and your marketing team owns the website, yes. It works best when a marketing lead agrees the priorities and someone in sales follows up on the enquiries. If it isn't a fit, I'll say so on the first call.

You'll see it early, in numbers. We record the baseline in weeks 1 and 2, and each monthly report shows search visits, enquiries and accepted leads against it. We work on one priority at a time, so when a change doesn't move the numbers we know which one it was and pick the next one together. I recheck the baseline at month 3 and month 6.

Nobody can promise a ranking or a mention, and I'd be wary of anyone who does. So I measure instead. I fix what stops search engines and AI tools from reading your site, write pages for the questions your buyers actually ask, and report what changed. Search visits and enquiries are measured numbers. AI mentions come from a dated sample of answers, reported separately, so you can see the trend.

Keep using it. I'll set the site up so your team can make changes with AI without breaking anything.
